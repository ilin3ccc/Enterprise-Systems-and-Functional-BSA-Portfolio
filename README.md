# Enterprise Systems & Functional BSA Portfolio

## Overview

This repository showcases end-to-end Business Systems Analysis (BSA), process improvement, solution design, delivery planning, and business intelligence artifacts.

Using a healthcare-focused ICU Early Warning System (EWS) case study, the portfolio demonstrates how business problems are analyzed, translated into requirements, transformed into solution designs, validated through delivery artifacts, measured through analytics, and communicated through strategic recommendations.

The focus is on traceability, documentation quality, structured analysis, and practical enterprise solutions.

---

## Portfolio Structure

### 01 - Business Analysis

Focuses on understanding the current state, identifying process inefficiencies, and performing root-cause analysis.

Current deliverables include:

- Current-State Swimlane Diagram
- Current-State BPMN Diagram
- Bottleneck Analysis

---

### 02 - Solution Design

Focuses on future-state process design, solution architecture, and requirements definition.

Current deliverables include:

- Future-State Swimlane Diagram
- Future-State BPMN Diagram
- Future-State Solution Architecture
- Business Requirements Document (BRD)
- Functional Specification Document (FSD)

---

### 03 - Delivery Planning

Focuses on transforming requirements into delivery-ready artifacts used by project teams, QA teams, and Agile delivery environments.

Current deliverables include:

- Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)
- Acceptance Criteria Catalogue

Planned deliverables:

### 04 - Analytics & Measurement

### 05 - Strategy & Communication
